Who we are

Poente is a fictional property agency made for this website. The licence number, addresses, telephone numbers and email addresses on these pages are placeholders.

Nothing here is an offer to sell a real property.

The homes

The ten houses are invented. Names of towns and regions are real and are used only to set the scene.

Areas, prices and descriptions are illustrative.

Light records

Sun positions are computed in your browser from latitude, date and hour using a standard solar declination formula. Times are solar time, so they can differ from the clock by up to about an hour and a half in summer.

The room in each light plan is a simplified model of seven by six metres. It does not include trees, neighbouring buildings or the thickness of walls.

Hours of sun are counted from the moment the sun is one and a half degrees above the horizon.

Photographs

Images on this site were generated with an AI image model. The people and buildings in them do not exist.

Visits

The form on the contact page opens a message in your own mail application. Nothing is sent to us until you send it.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts. It uses session storage in your browser to remember that you have seen the opening sequence, and forgets it when you close the tab.

Accessibility

Motion is reduced when your system asks for it. Every control works from the keyboard, including the sun in the opening picture.

If something does not work for you, write to hello@poente.example.
