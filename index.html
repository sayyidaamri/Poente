What we do
Sell, find and value houses in Portugal, with a light record for each

Poente is the Portuguese word for where the sun goes down. Agents here write it on a listing to mean the west side. We took it as a name because it is the one fact about a house that no renovation can change.

In the file

What we write down

  • Which way the main room facesTaken with a compass at the window, not from the deed
  • Sun on 21 June and 21 DecemberHours of direct light, computed, then checked on site
  • What is in the wayTrees, chimneys, the neighbour's new storey

What we leave out

  • The words bright and spaciousYou will see for yourself
  • Photographs lit with lampsIf it was taken at four, it says four
  • Offers before three visitsOne of them at the wrong hour

A room is a slow sundial. Once you can read one, you stop asking about square metres.

From window
to floor

The drawing above is not an illustration. It takes the latitude, the date and the hour, works out where the sun is, and projects each window along that line until it meets the floor or a wall. We do it for the main room of every house on our list. You can move the hour yourself on each page.

Three paths
in a year

In June the sun rises well to the north-east and passes almost overhead. In December it stays low, and reaches four times further into a room. A house that is perfect in one month can be a cave or an oven in another, so we show all three.
