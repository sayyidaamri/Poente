White cubes stacked to a roof terrace, with the salt pans beyond. The brightest house on our list, and the one that taught us to bring sunglasses to a valuation.

Light plan

A house for
the middle of the day

Olhão was built by fishermen who had seen North Africa. Stairs run outside, roofs are rooms, and the lime-wash is done every spring. Inside, the walls are bare and slightly curved, and one oculus in the ceiling draws a disc that crosses the bench at noon.

The terrace looks over working salt pans that go pink in late summer. The neighbours keep pigeons. There is a covenant on the skyline, so nobody will build up in front of you.
