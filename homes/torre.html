A schist tower above the terraces, four rooms stacked one on another. The valley bends so that the last sun comes up the river and in through the bronze windows.

07:00

Light plan

A house for
the evening

Each floor has a different light. The kitchen is at the bottom, half in the hill and cool. The double-height room is above it and takes the afternoon. The bedroom at the top has both ends of the day and no curtains, because nobody can see in.

The vines are let to the neighbour and the lease runs to 2034. Harvest is loud for nine days in September. The pool is a single lane against the terrace wall, and it warms by itself from May.
