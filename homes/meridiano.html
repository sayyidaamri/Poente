A long white house with its back to the pines and two walls to the sun. The main room has the first light on one side, the last on the other, and a reed pergola that takes the edge off noon.

07:00

Light plan

A house for
the middle of the day

The house is one room deep and forty metres long. That is an expensive way to build and it is the reason to buy it: every room has a window on both sides, and no corridor is dark. The living room sits at the bend, so it gets the sun-path of two walls instead of one.

In August the pergola does real work. The reeds are cut to let through about a third of the light, and the concrete floor stays cool underfoot until four. In December the same low sun passes beneath them and reaches the back wall.

Its best hour

10:00

Sun on both floors of the long room, and the pines still holding the wind.

Its worst hour

15:00

In July the terrace is too bright to read on. Stand there before you decide.
