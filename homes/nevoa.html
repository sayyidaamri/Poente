A granite manor on the wet side of the hill. The main rooms face north and see almost no direct sun, and the people who ask for it ask for exactly that.

07:00

A house for

North light is even, shadowless and kind to paper. The library was built by a botanist who pressed ferns, and the tall windows are there to show green as it is. Nothing in this room has faded since 1891.

Sintra makes its own weather. On around a hundred days of the year the mist sits below the terrace until eleven, and the house seems to float on it. The boiler is new. You will use it.

Its best hour

The mist lifts past the camellias and the gravel dries.

In December it is nearly dark. Three lamps and a fire are part of the house.
